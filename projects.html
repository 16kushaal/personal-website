spark : sustainable power analytics and renewable kinetics

2025

spark is a modular energy analytics platform leveraging hadoop and apache Spark to process and forecast large-scale renewable energy data


• delivers accurate short- and long-term load predictions with 95%+ accuracy, seasonal pattern analysis, and demand-weather correlations to enhance grid reliability.

• provides deep insights into renewable and non-renewable generation trends, efficiency, and fossil fuel dependency for environmental impact and strategic evaluation.

• implements a distributed architecture with hadoop and spark on separate isolated docker container stacks.
